# Product Vision and Scope

## 1. Vision

Build an interactive AI learning and knowledge platform where students, beginners, developers and working professionals can understand artificial intelligence, experiment with its concepts and build real applications.

The platform is not simply a blog or a conventional learning management system. It combines structured education, visual learning, practical coding, AI resources and career development.

## 2. Positioning

**Learn difficult things the easy way.**

Supporting promise:

**See it. Understand it. Experiment with it. Build it.**

The platform should feel approachable to a complete beginner while offering a meaningful progression into advanced engineering topics.

## 3. Primary audiences

### Beginners and students
People who want to understand AI, learn Python, explore machine learning and prepare for further study or employment.

### Developers
People who want to build AI-powered applications, work with APIs, create agents and deploy AI systems.

### Working professionals
People who want to understand AI tools, automate workflows and apply AI in their roles.

### Educators and institutions
People who want clear learning resources, workshops and structured AI education.

## 4. Product areas

### AI Academy
Structured schools, learning paths, modules, lessons, quizzes and progress.

### Interactive Labs
Visualizations, simulations, coding exercises and guided experiments.

### AI Knowledge Hub
Articles, explainers, research breakdowns, tutorials and educational resources.

### AI Tools Directory
Curated AI tools organized by task, category, pricing model and use case. Tool information must be reviewed and dated.

### Project Lab
Guided projects with prerequisites, source code, milestones and expected outcomes.

### AI Career Hub
Career roadmaps, interview preparation, portfolio guidance and internship resources.

## 5. Product principles

1. Beginner-friendly does not mean technically shallow.
2. Explain the intuition before introducing dense terminology.
3. Use visualizations when they genuinely improve comprehension.
4. Let learners manipulate concepts rather than only watch them.
5. Connect theory to implementation and real-world applications.
6. Make foundational learning genuinely useful in the free tier.
7. Keep content current, referenced and reviewable.
8. Make accessibility and responsive design standard requirements.
9. Do not use gamification as a substitute for learning.
10. Prioritize learning outcomes over content volume.

## 6. Scope boundaries for the first release

### In scope
- Public website and brand pages
- AI Academy with school, path, module and lesson pages
- Reusable lesson renderer
- Initial interactive visualizations
- Quizzes and basic progress tracking
- Student accounts
- Admin content management
- Free and premium content flags

### Out of scope initially
- Public discussion forums
- Complex social profiles
- Native mobile applications
- A large multi-tenant institutional LMS
- Unrestricted user-generated code execution
- Automated certificate accreditation
- A custom foundation model

These can be evaluated after the first release has real users and usage data.
